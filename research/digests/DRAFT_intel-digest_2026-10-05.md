## Top 3 this week

### 1. Meta launched a free AI assistant that reads your actual Instagram and Facebook data

- **What changed:** On 29 September 2026, Meta launched "Muse for Small Business," an AI assistant built directly into Meta Business Suite. It connects to your Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, ad account, and linked tools including Shopify, Klaviyo, Canva, and QuickBooks. You ask it plain-language questions about your performance, and it answers from your real data, not generic advice. "What content performed best this month?" gets a real answer, not a guess.
- **Source + date:** CNBC, 29 September 2026 (cnbc.com/2026/09/29/meta-launches-muse-for-small-business-zuckerberg-pushes-enterprise-ai.html). Note: direct page access was restricted by network policy; date and details confirmed via CNBC coverage and corroborating sources.
- **So what for the owner:** The weekly performance question ("what content is actually working?") no longer requires exporting from Instagram Insights, importing into a spreadsheet, and drawing your own conclusions. Ask Muse and it tells you, in plain language, from your account data. The Canva integration means it can go from "here is your best-performing post format" to drafting the next one in the same session. This is free inside Meta Business Suite.
- **Honest caveat:** Muse is part of Meta's new "Meta One" subscription tiers, so some features may sit behind a paid plan depending on your market. AI recommendations should be reviewed before acting on them, especially for paid ads, where an automated suggestion can spend real money quickly. Availability outside the US is not yet confirmed. *(Check Meta Business Suite for your market and tier.)*
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Your Instagram account can now answer its own performance questions. Here is what changed."

### 2. Instagram now turns a still photo into a moving reel, inside the Edits app

- **What changed:** On 30 September 2026, Instagram rolled out an AI video assistant inside its free Edits app for creators. The headline feature: motion effects that animate a still photo into a short video clip. A good photo of your product, your space, or your face can become a reel-ready post without filming new footage. Instagram is also testing per-slide captions on carousel posts, where each slide gets its own caption text.
- **Source + date:** TechCrunch, 30 September 2026 (techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/instagram-rolls-out-an-ai-video-assistant-for-creators/).
- **So what for the owner:** Reels get more reach than static posts on Instagram right now. The main barrier for most small owners is time to film. Motion AI removes that barrier: your existing library of good photos becomes usable reel content. A yoga studio's class-atmosphere shot, a health practitioner's clinic space, a product brand's flat-lay photography, all become moving content without a camera or a script.
- **Honest caveat:** The motion effects are in the Edits app (free download from the App Store and Google Play), not inside the main Instagram app. Per-slide carousel captions are still in testing, so not yet available to everyone. Motion AI works best on photos with clear subjects; abstract or very busy images may produce odd results. *(Regulated-claim flag: for wellness owners, avoid using motion AI on anything depicting health outcomes, before-after transformations, or clinical settings.)*
- **Content angle:** Reel or single · Proof and news · hook: "You do not need new footage. Your existing photos can become Reels now."

### 3. Google Workspace now lets you save AI instructions permanently, across every app

- **What changed:** Google Gemini in Workspace received "Skills" — reusable prompt templates stored permanently in your account — beginning to roll out in Workspace apps on 5 October 2026. You write an instruction once: "generate my monthly client newsletter in this structure," "reply to new enquiries in my warm, plain-English tone." Gemini applies it every time, in Gmail, Docs, and Meet, without you re-typing it.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog (workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/09/skills-gemini-app-workspace.html), announced September 2026, Workspace rollout confirmed 5 October 2026. Note: direct page fetch restricted by network policy; confirmed via multiple independent sources citing the same blog post, including Jetstream.blog and Google's own September 2026 update summary.
- **So what for the owner:** The biggest friction with AI tools is starting every session from scratch, re-explaining your business, your tone, your format. Skills close that gap. A therapist, coach, or clinic owner on Google Workspace can set a Skill for each recurring task and have Gemini pick it up automatically. If you are already paying for Workspace, this is included at no extra cost.
- **Honest caveat:** Rolling out gradually through October, so it may not appear on your account on day one. Requires a paid Google Workspace subscription; standard free Gmail accounts are not included. The existing Gems feature is being phased out and transitioned to Skills.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Stop re-explaining yourself to AI every session. Workspace just made it a one-time setup."
