## Top 3 this week

### 1. Xero JAX now predicts when your clients will pay their invoices

- **What changed:** Xero's built-in AI assistant, Just Ask Xero (JAX), can now forecast when each outstanding invoice is likely to be paid, based on that client's past payment patterns. It then automatically sends personalised payment reminders timed to each client's actual behaviour. A client who ignores texts but responds to email gets an email statement; a chronic late-payer gets a reminder earlier. Expense claim processing is also automated.
- **Source + date:** Xero (xero.com/jax / Xero Central release notes), confirmed September 3, 2026. Reported across TechRepublic ("Xero's JAX Now Smarter at Predicting When Customers Will Pay"), Releasebot, and Accounting Today, all citing September 3.
- **So what for the owner:** Less time chasing unpaid invoices. Your accounting software now does the follow-up for you, personalised per client, without you writing a single reminder. For a clinic, studio, or coaching practice where late payments are a persistent cash-flow stress, this removes a weekly admin task.
- **Honest caveat:** You need a Xero subscription to access JAX. Predictions improve with payment history, so new clients get less reliable forecasts. This is a cash-flow and admin tool, not financial or tax advice. Regulated-claim flag: frame all finance content around time and admin wins, not financial outcomes.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · Finance/Admin lane · hook: "Your accounting tool now knows which clients are going to pay late. Here is what JAX does about it."

### 2. Gemini in Google Workspace now does tasks across all your apps at once

- **What changed:** Google unified the Gemini assistant across all Workspace apps (Gmail, Docs, Drive, Slides, Chat, Calendar) so it has the same capabilities everywhere. You can now ask Gemini to schedule a meeting, send an email, or create a task from whichever app you happen to be working in, without switching tabs. For example, from a Google Doc you can ask: "Send this summary to my team for review" and Gemini drafts and sends the email, without you opening Gmail.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog (workspaceupdates.googleblog.com), reported September 9, 2026 by 9to5Google. Official Google Workspace blog post titled: "Create content, schedule events, and coordinate tasks across Workspace regardless of what app you are in."
- **So what for the owner:** Stop context-switching. If you run your client communications, scheduling, and documents inside Google Workspace, you can now ask one place to handle tasks across all of them. "Book a follow-up with the client in this email" or "add this to my task list" from wherever you already are.
- **Honest caveat:** Requires a paid Google Workspace plan that includes Gemini (Business or Enterprise tier). Not available on the free Gmail or personal Google account. Capabilities are still rolling out and may not reach all accounts immediately.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · Productivity/Operations lane · hook: "You can now ask Google to do 5 things at once. From the same tab you are already in."

### 3. HubSpot's Breeze AI invoice ranking is now free for all accounts

- **What changed:** HubSpot's Breeze AI feature that ranks your unpaid invoices by revenue impact and drafts personalised collection emails has graduated from beta to general availability. It is now included on all HubSpot hubs and tiers, including free accounts. You open your invoice list, Breeze surfaces the highest-value outstanding invoices first, and has a draft collection email ready to send.
- **Source + date:** HubSpot release notes (hubspot.com/changelog), confirmed September 2026. Reported by Releasebot (HubSpot September 2026 updates) and multiple HubSpot partner blogs citing the GA date.
- **So what for the owner:** If you already use HubSpot's free CRM for client management, you now have AI-prioritised invoice chasing built in, at no extra cost. It tells you which unpaid invoices to focus on this week, and gets the follow-up email written.
- **Honest caveat:** Only useful if you use HubSpot for invoicing and billing. Owners using Xero, QuickBooks, or Wave for accounting may find Xero JAX (item 1 above) more relevant. The email drafts still need your review before sending. Regulated-claim flag: frame as admin and time savings, not financial advice.
- **Content angle:** Single image or short carousel · Admin/CRM lane · hook: "HubSpot now tells you which invoices to chase first. And writes the email."
