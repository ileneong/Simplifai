## Top 3 this week

### 1. ChatGPT free plan now lets you set up automations that run on their own

- **What changed:** On 25 August 2026, OpenAI rolled out scheduled tasks to ChatGPT's free plan, with a limit of three tasks per account. Free users can now set a task to run on a schedule and come back to the result. Plus and Pro users got an upgrade at the same time: their tasks can now trigger automatically when something changes in Gmail, Slack, or GitHub, rather than only firing on a set clock schedule.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes (help.openai.com), 25 August 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** If you are on the free plan, you now have three slots to put recurring admin on autopilot. You could set one to draft your weekly check-in email every Monday morning, one to surface your most urgent to-do on Friday afternoons, and one to remind you to post before the week closes. You do not need to log in or remember. It simply happens. For a wellness owner running between appointments, this is three fewer things to hold in their head. Plus and Pro users get the more powerful version: a task can kick off the moment a specific client email lands, without any clock at all.
- **Honest caveat:** The free plan caps you at three tasks, so choose carefully. Trigger-based tasks (the ones that fire on a Gmail or Slack event rather than a schedule) are Plus and Pro only, at $20 or $200 per month respectively. The tasks that run on a plain clock schedule are the ones the free plan gets. Also, tasks still require you to phrase the instruction clearly, and they do not have memory of what happened in previous runs unless you build that into the prompt.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "ChatGPT just gave free users 3 automations. Here is what to do with yours."

### 2. Google Workspace owners can now ask Gemini questions from inside Google Chat

- **What changed:** Starting 26 August 2026, Google began rolling out Ask Gemini in Google Chat, which puts a Gemini-powered assistant directly inside the Chat interface. From a single Chat window, owners can search across their Gmail, Drive, and Calendar in plain English, generate images, draft updates, manage tasks, and schedule meetings, all without switching apps.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog (workspaceupdates.googleblog.com), 26 August 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** If you and your team run on Google Workspace, this means the question "what time did we say we were meeting the supplier?" no longer requires opening Gmail, scrolling through threads, and then going back to your Chat. You ask it in Chat and get the answer back there. For a clinic owner coordinating bookings, staff schedules, and client emails across Google tools, this cuts the tab-switching loop significantly. Promotional higher usage limits are available through 1 October 2026.
- **Honest caveat:** This requires a Google Workspace Business Standard plan or higher (not available on free Google accounts or the entry-level Business Starter plan). It is rolling out gradually, so it may not appear on your account immediately. Do not type patient names, health details, or any personal client information into Gemini Chat; keep queries factual and general, and retrieve sensitive details yourself. *(Regulated-claim flag: health/privacy context for wellness owners.)*
- **Content angle:** Single or Carousel · System teaching · hook: "One place. All your Google tools. Here is what changed in Workspace this week."

### 3. QuickBooks can now draft your invoices from a conversation

- **What changed:** QuickBooks' August 2026 update brought invoice creation through Intuit Intelligence chat to QuickBooks Online. Owners can describe a project in plain language, paste in notes from a spreadsheet, or upload a supporting document, and Intuit Intelligence drafts the invoice. The same update added an AI-powered Payments Agent designed to accelerate how quickly invoices are sent and followed up on.
- **Source + date:** QuickBooks Blog, quickbooks.intuit.com ("What's new in QuickBooks Online, August 2026"), August 2026. No exact day published at time of research.
- **So what for the owner:** Service-based owners who invoice clients, coaches, therapists, consultants, trades, anyone building an invoice from their own notes, can now describe what they did ("three 60-minute sessions in August, plus a meal plan") and receive a drafted invoice to review. It does not remove the review step, but it shortens the blank-page moment significantly.
- **Honest caveat:** This requires a QuickBooks Online subscription. The Payments Agent's speed claim ("up to four times faster") comes from QuickBooks' own marketing and has not been independently verified; treat it as directional, not guaranteed. As always, review every invoice before sending it and consult your accountant for anything touching tax or compliance. *(Regulated-claim flag: finance. Frame any resulting post as a time and admin win, not as financial or tax advice.)*
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Describe your project. QuickBooks drafts the invoice. Here is how that works."
