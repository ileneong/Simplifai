## Top 3 this week

### 1. Claude now does spreadsheets, whiteboards, and meetings
- **What changed:** Anthropic shipped a cluster of workplace tools inside Claude: Claude Sheets (build and edit spreadsheets from a plain-English ask, beta), Claude Whiteboards (brainstorm visually, beta), Claude Data (shareable dashboards, now generally available), plus Claude Meetings (add Claude to a video call, announced 12 Nov) and Personal Finance (ask about money across connected accounts, 5 Nov).
- **Source + date:** Anthropic, claude.com blog announcements, 5–12 November 2026 (verified directly on the blog listing).
- **So what for the owner:** the tool many owners already use for writing can now also hold the price list, the weekly numbers sheet, and the planning board. One subscription, fewer tools to learn. A studio owner can ask for a class-revenue sheet in plain English instead of fighting formulas.
- **Honest caveat:** Sheets and Whiteboards are beta and paid-plan only (no free tier). Secondary coverage reports Sheets caps around 100,000 cells, has no pivot tables or macros, and cannot pull live data from connected apps yet. Everything counts against the plan's normal usage limits. Meetings is rolling out, not universally available.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Your AI assistant just learned spreadsheets."

### 2. Gemini "skills" roll out across Google Workspace
- **What changed:** Google began rolling out reusable Gemini skills (saved, repeatable AI instructions) across Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive and Chat, and expanded Gemini in Gmail (thread summaries, drafting) to all Workspace users aged 18+.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog, 10 November 2026, rollout phases from 9 and 30 November. *Verification note: the Workspace blog is outside this session's allowed network, so dates come from consistent secondary coverage of the official post; re-verify before publishing.*
- **So what for the owner:** owners already paying for Google Workspace get a way to save their best prompts (a booking-reply skill, a newsletter-draft skill) and reuse them inside the inbox and docs they already live in, no new tool or tab.
- **Honest caveat:** gradual rollout (up to 15 days to appear), not yet in Sheets or Meet, and skills do not sync with Gems in the Gemini app. Admins can switch it off, so some owners on managed domains will not see it.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Stop retyping your best prompts into Gmail."

### 3. ChatGPT Images 2.6 ships faster, more consistent image edits
- **What changed:** OpenAI released Images 2.6 in ChatGPT: a new image model that generates faster and holds details steady across repeated edits.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes, 4 November 2026. *Verification note: help.openai.com is outside this session's allowed network; date comes from the release-notes page as surfaced in search. Re-verify before publishing.*
- **So what for the owner:** edit-consistency is the practical win. An owner can generate a product or promo image, then ask for small changes (swap the text, change the background) without the whole image morphing, which is what made earlier AI images unusable for brand work.
- **Honest caveat:** plan availability and rate limits were not confirmed at the primary source this week. AI product imagery still needs a disclosure habit and a check for accuracy before it represents a real product.
- **Content angle:** Reel or single · Proof / news · hook: "AI images finally stopped redrawing your whole design."
