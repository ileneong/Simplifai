## Top 3 this week

### 1. ChatGPT's dictation is now on every plan, no time limit

- **What changed:** ChatGPT's Live Dictation feature rolled out globally on 18 November to all plans (including free), on iOS, Android, web, and desktop. Text appears on screen as you speak, in real time. The previous 10-minute recording cap is gone.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI release notes (help.openai.com/release-notes), 18 November 2026. Coverage: Digital Trends, AI Weekly, Cryptobriefing -- all consistent on the all-plans rollout and cap removal.
- **So what for the owner:** You can now speak instead of type, for as long as you need, on any device. For a wellness owner, that means narrating a client session summary on the walk back to your desk, speaking out a week of captions while you're between appointments, or dictating a full enquiry reply hands-free. Typing to ChatGPT is now optional.
- **Honest caveat:** Dictation is a typing replacement, not Voice mode (the conversational back-and-forth). It transcribes your words; it does not respond. If you have opted in to audio training, OpenAI may use dictation audio to improve its models -- check Settings > "Improve the model for everyone" to control this. No date given for when "rolling out globally" completes on all platforms.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "You can now talk your way through your whole business."

### 2. HubSpot: using Claude or ChatGPT with your CRM is now in your seat price

- **What changed:** HubSpot announced on 17-18 November that everyday work done through Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot with your HubSpot CRM is covered by your existing paid seat. The connector, MCP server, and Agent CLI come included at no extra charge.
- **Source + date:** HubSpot company blog, 17-18 November 2026 (via ppc.land and smtpedia.com, citing HubSpot's post by product lead Duncan Lennox). Check hubspot.com/spotlight for the official announcement.
- **So what for the owner:** If you have any paid HubSpot seat, you can now ask Claude or ChatGPT to find a contact, pull up your pipeline, draft a follow-up, or review your bookings data -- without being billed extra for the AI connection. The CRM and the AI tool you already pay for now talk to each other by default.
- **Honest caveat:** "Everyday work" is covered; heavy agent runs (bulk lead enrichment, personalised outreach at scale) still use HubSpot Credits ($0.01 each beyond the monthly allowance). HubSpot has not published a precise definition of what counts as everyday use, so test before committing to heavy workflows. Free CRM users are not included -- this applies to paid seats only.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Your CRM and your AI tool just officially became a team."

### 3. Canva now lets you turn off generative AI features with one switch

- **What changed:** Canva Free and Pro users gained two new controls on 19 November. A toggle under Profile > Settings > Permissions hides all generative AI tools across Canva (Magic Write, Magic Design, Dream Lab, Magic Edit, and others). A second toggle reduces AI-generated content in search results and recommendations.
- **Source + date:** Canva, 19 November 2026. Covered by Petapixel, 9to5Mac, and unboxdailyhq.com -- all date it 19 November 2026 and confirm it is available on Free and Pro at no extra cost.
- **So what for the owner:** If you work in a regulated field, serve clients who prefer human-made creative, or simply want a cleaner interface without generative tools in the way, you now have that choice. The toggle is free, reversible, and takes about five seconds. Assistive tools (Background Remover, Magic Grab) stay on.
- **Honest caveat:** The toggle hides generative tools; it does not change Canva's data-training setting, which must be adjusted separately in Privacy settings. Some countries were unable to access the new controls at launch (reported: India, Philippines, Indonesia). Check whether both controls appear in your account.
- **[REGULATED-CLAIM FLAG]** For health and wellness owners creating health content in Canva: this toggle can help ensure no AI-generated health claims appear in your designs without review. Worth knowing even if you don't use it.
- **Content angle:** Single or Reel · Honest limits / Reframe · hook: "You can now turn off AI in Canva. Here's when you should."
