## Top 3 this week

### 1. Meta AI can now read your actual Instagram and Facebook data

- **What changed:** Meta AI (the assistant at meta.ai) can now connect to your Facebook and Instagram accounts, your Meta Ads campaigns, and your Google Workspace files (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides). Instead of working from generic advice, it can pull your actual engagement numbers and give answers about your specific business.
- **Source + date:** Meta, announced across multiple outlets including Social Media Today, Small Biz Trends, and Dataconomy, 20 August 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** Ask Meta AI "which posts got the most saves last month?" and get answers from your real account data, not guesswork. You can also set up a recurring weekly report on your Instagram performance, compare your organic results with similar brands in your category, and ask it to build a Google Sheet summary, all inside one conversation.
- **Honest caveat:** Rolling out across meta.ai web, mobile app, and desktop app, but not all accounts will have full access immediately. The ad analytics tools are more developed than the organic-content tools right now. Linking Google Workspace requires a separate one-time account connection. The benchmarking uses publicly available content from comparable brands, so comparisons are directional, not exact.
- **Regulated-claim flag:** This tool analyzes engagement metrics only. If you are in health, finance, or legal, the AI's content recommendations will be based on what performed well, not on what is compliant; apply your own professional guardrails before acting on suggestions.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Meta AI can now read your own Instagram numbers. Here is what to ask it first."

### 2. ChatGPT can now open and edit your Google Drive files without leaving the chat

- **What changed:** ChatGPT added Google Drive to its Library (the file panel inside the chat). Paid users can @mention any Drive file to pull it into the conversation, and Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides can stay open side-by-side with the chat window on the web. No more copying and pasting between tabs.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI, rolled out 13-14 August 2026. Reported by 9to5Mac, MacObserver, and Progressive Robot (14 August 2026).
- **So what for the owner:** Pull up your existing client welcome doc, your service menu, or your newsletter draft with one @mention and ask ChatGPT to tighten it, translate it, or turn it into a checklist, all without switching apps. Works with files already in your Drive, so there is nothing to re-upload.
- **Honest caveat:** Paid plans only (ChatGPT Plus at US$20 per month minimum; also Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu). Web version only, mobile support is planned but not yet available. Two-way sync is limited: ChatGPT can show and suggest edits, but you will need to apply changes back to your Drive file yourself.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Stop copying and pasting. ChatGPT can now open your Google Docs directly."

### 3. Google Chat gets an AI command that searches your whole Workspace at once

- **What changed:** Google is adding Ask Gemini to Google Chat, a command that lets Workspace users search across Gmail, Drive, and Calendar, draft messages, generate images, and manage tasks without switching between apps. Type your request in Chat and Gemini goes across your whole account to find, draft, or do it.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog, announced August 2026, rolling out from 26 August 2026. (Promotional higher limits through 1 October 2026; standard usage limits apply after.)
- **So what for the owner:** If your practice or business runs on Google Workspace, you can now find a client email, draft a reply, and check your calendar without leaving Google Chat. Useful for reducing the tab-switching that breaks focus during a busy clinic or studio day.
- **Honest caveat:** Google Workspace paid subscribers only, this does not apply to free Gmail accounts. Rolling out from 26 August, so it may not be in your account yet. Usage limits will apply after the promotional period ends on 1 October 2026. Not yet confirmed whether solo Business Starter plans or Workspace Essentials will have the same access as higher tiers.
- **Content angle:** Single image or Reel · News/reframe · hook: "Google Chat just got a lot more useful (if you are on Workspace)."
