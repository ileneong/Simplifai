## Top 3 this week

### 1. Google Workspace now connects Gemini directly to QuickBooks, HubSpot, and Mailchimp

- **What changed:** Google added Model Context Protocol connections from Gemini (the AI layer inside Gmail, Docs, and Sheets) to QuickBooks, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Asana, Monday.com, and Salesforce. You can ask questions about your finances, update contacts, or check campaign data without leaving your Google tools.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates Blog, 18 September 2026 (workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/09/weekly-recap-09-18-2026.html)
- **So what for the owner:** If you already use Google Workspace and QuickBooks or HubSpot, you can now ask Gemini inside Gmail "What invoices are outstanding this month?" and get an answer without logging into QuickBooks separately. One less app-switch for routine finance and CRM checks every day.
- **Honest caveat:** Requires a paid Google Workspace plan (Business Starter or above) with the Gemini add-on active, plus an existing QuickBooks or HubSpot account. Account connections are managed through Google's admin console, which can feel technical if you haven't been in there before. Free Gmail accounts are not supported.
- **Content angle:** Carousel (how-to) · System teaching · hook: "Your Gmail just got a direct line to your bookkeeping."

### 2. ChatGPT's voice mode now activates your connected apps, hands-free

- **What changed:** ChatGPT's live voice mode on iOS, Android, and web now works with plugins and connected apps. You can speak to ChatGPT and have it summarize emails, check calendar appointments, or draft a client reply, all without touching your keyboard.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI release notes, 23 September 2026 (help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)
- **So what for the owner:** Between appointments, in the car, or while tidying up, you can now talk through your inbox out loud. Say "What client messages came in today?" and ChatGPT reads them back and offers to draft a reply. Less screen time for routine comms, without losing any of the thinking work.
- **Honest caveat:** Voice with connected apps requires a paid ChatGPT plan (Plus, Team, or Work). Free-tier voice access remains more limited. Your apps (Gmail, Calendar) need to be connected to ChatGPT once in settings before this works, which takes about 10 minutes.
- **Content angle:** Carousel (how-to) · System teaching · hook: "Your phone is now your business assistant. No typing required."

### 3. QuickBooks now suggests a category for every transaction so you start from done, not scratch

- **What changed:** QuickBooks shipped its September 2026 product update, including AI that categorizes incoming transactions and flags unusual ones for your review. The update also adds support for multiple shipping addresses per client on invoices.
- **Source + date:** QuickBooks product update blog, September 2026 (quickbooks.intuit.com/r/product-update/whats-new-quickbooks-online-september-2026/)
- **So what for the owner:** Monthly bookkeeping moves faster. Instead of categorizing every transaction from scratch, you review and approve the AI's suggestions, spending time only on the ones it is unsure about. Multi-address invoices help anyone who bills the same client at different locations.
- **Honest caveat:** AI categorization is a starting point, not a guarantee. You still need to review its work, especially for anything unusual or mixed-use. This is a time win in admin, not a substitute for a bookkeeper or accountant on complex matters. REGULATED-CLAIM FLAG: frame as admin time saved only, not financial or tax advice.
- **Content angle:** Carousel (system teaching) · System teaching · hook: "Your bookkeeping app now does the first pass for you. Here's what that means for your week."
