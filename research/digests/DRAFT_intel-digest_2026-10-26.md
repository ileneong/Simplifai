## Top 3 this week

### 1. Canva launches its own AI image generator, Craft

- **What changed:** Canva released Craft, its first in-house AI image model, inside Dream Lab on October 22, 2026. Available worldwide immediately to all Canva users (free and paid). It generates images in 30+ aspect ratios, supports text in 55 languages, and outputs up to 2K resolution. It works in two steps: it sketches a layout first (to keep text readable and objects well-placed), then generates the final image. Resulting images can be converted into editable elements via Magic Layers. A public API also launched for developers.
- **Source + date:** Canva newsroom, October 22, 2026. Cross-confirmed by TechCrunch, BetaNews, and WinBuzzer on October 22-23, 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** A wellness studio, coach, or clinic can now generate on-brand social graphics, flyers, and client handouts without leaving Canva or paying for a separate AI image tool. Because Craft was trained on Canva's own template library, results should slot into existing Canva designs without friction. Dream Lab is in the Canva app now.
- **Honest caveat:** In independent image quality rankings (DesignArena), Craft placed 4th out of roughly six tested models. It was trained on graphic templates, not photography, so photorealistic images of people may be weaker than Midjourney or DALL-E. Promised features like "reference images" and more editing controls are planned but not yet shipped.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · Saves · Hook: "Canva just built its own AI image maker. Here's what it means for your business."

### 2. Notion's agents can now run for more than 24 hours

- **What changed:** Notion rebuilt its Agent and Custom Agents on October 19, 2026 (called the Gen-6 harness). A single task can now run autonomously for 24 hours or more, up from roughly 5 minutes. Agents get their own browser and computer. A new "Review for me" safety mode pauses before committing irreversible actions and asks the owner to check first. An upgraded model router delivers near-frontier quality at around 90% lower credit cost. Business and Enterprise workspaces get 1,500 Standard and 500 Select credits per month; existing workspaces switch to the new credit system on November 2, 2026.
- **Source + date:** Notion release notes (notion.com/releases/2026-10-19), October 19, 2026. Cross-confirmed by Forkast News and WebProNews.
- **So what for the owner:** A wellness owner who already uses Notion can now set an agent running on a multi-step task (compile a client progress report, research suppliers, draft a week of newsletters, process intake responses) and come back to a finished result. "Review for me" mode means the agent asks before taking irreversible actions, which is practical for owners who do not want to micromanage AI.
- **Honest caveat:** The new credit system limits monthly usage, and heavy users may hit caps. The feature is available on Business and Enterprise plans. AI Command Center is still listed as coming soon. Verify your plan's credit allowance at notion.com before building autonomous workflows your business depends on.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · Saves · Hook: "Your Notion can now work while you're with clients. Here's how."

### 3. ChatGPT can now build and co-edit presentations

- **What changed:** ChatGPT added a Slides feature on October 16, 2026. Users can create presentations directly inside ChatGPT: edit text, images, shapes, and charts, then export to PowerPoint, PDF, or Google Slides. Teammates can co-edit in real time. Available on the web.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI release notes (help.openai.com/release-notes), October 16, 2026. Cross-confirmed by MacRumors and Releasebot.
- **So what for the owner:** A coach or practitioner can now take a ChatGPT-drafted session outline, program proposal, or client resource and turn it into a shareable presentation in the same tool, then send it as a PowerPoint or Google Slides file. No separate app needed for simple client decks.
- **Honest caveat:** Available on the web only (not confirmed on mobile). The plan tier required is not confirmed in available sources, likely Plus or above. Co-editing requires teammates to also have ChatGPT accounts. For polished, branded presentations, Canva still leads. This is best for functional drafts and client-facing documents that prioritise content over design.
- **Content angle:** Single post or 2-slide carousel · Proof/news · Reach · Hook: "ChatGPT now builds slides. Here's what that means for your practice."
