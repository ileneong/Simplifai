## Top 3 this week

### 1. ChatGPT now remembers your brand assets between sessions

- **What changed:** OpenAI shipped Images 2.6 on November 4, 2026. It introduces saved reference images: upload a photo once (your logo, a product shot, a room photo), give it a name, and ChatGPT recalls it in any future session without you re-attaching it. The same update adds GIF creation from text prompts.
- **Source + date:** OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes (openai.com/products/release-notes), 4 November 2026
- **So what for the owner:** A clinic owner can upload their logo once and reference it by name whenever they want ChatGPT to include it in a graphic or mockup. A coach can save a headshot and generate consistent branded visuals without re-uploading every session. The GIF feature lets any owner create a short looping animation (product demo, exercise tip, before/after movement) directly in ChatGPT.
- **Honest caveat:** Free-plan access is not confirmed in available sources. This may require a ChatGPT Plus or Pro subscription. GIF quality and file size for social posts should be tested before use. Best suited for quick-and-share visuals rather than polished production work.
- **Content angle:** Carousel (how-to) · System teaching · hook: "Save your logo in ChatGPT once. It remembers it forever."

### 2. HubSpot Breeze can now run your AI prompts on a schedule

- **What changed:** HubSpot's Breeze Assistant added Scheduled Prompts. Set any AI prompt to run daily, weekly, or monthly and it fires automatically, delivering the result without you needing to log in and ask.
- **Source + date:** HubSpot Fall 2026 Spotlight (hubspot.com/company-news/fall-26-spotlight); HubSpot knowledge base updated 4 November 2026
- **So what for the owner:** A wellness studio owner could set Breeze to generate a Friday summary of new enquiries every week, or a monthly snapshot of active contacts, without touching HubSpot at all. It is the difference between using AI when you remember to and having it do the work while you are with clients.
- **Honest caveat:** Exact ship date and minimum plan tier could not be confirmed from available sources. The feature appeared in the developer changelog under the Fall 2026 Spotlight, which suggests recent availability, but owners should verify it is live on their plan before building workflows around it. Regulated-claim flag: any scheduled automation that processes health client information must comply with applicable privacy regulations (AU Privacy Act, HIPAA where relevant). Do not create prompts that surface or export identifiable patient or client health data.
- **Content angle:** Single post or Reel (reframe) · Honest limits · hook: "AI that works while you are with clients."

### 3. Microsoft Copilot in Excel can now clean your messy data with one ask

- **What changed:** Microsoft is rolling out three new Copilot skills for Excel in November 2026: a clean data skill (finds and fixes inconsistencies in spreadsheets), accounting skills (ask plain-English questions about financial data), and an interactive canvas for conversational data work.
- **Source + date:** Microsoft, "What's New in Microsoft Copilot, October 2026" (techcommunity.microsoft.com), listed for November worldwide rollout
- **So what for the owner:** The clean data skill is the most immediately useful for a solo owner. If you keep client lists, booking records, or stock data in Excel and they are inconsistent (mixed date formats, duplicate entries, mismatched names), Copilot can find and fix them in seconds. The accounting skills mean you can ask "what did I spend on supplies this quarter?" in plain English and get an answer, without building a formula.
- **Honest caveat:** This is rolling out through November, so it may not be available in all tenants yet. It requires a Microsoft 365 Business plan with the Copilot add-on, which is an additional paid subscription on top of M365. Not available on personal or home plans. Regulated-claim flag: if the spreadsheet contains health client records, review Microsoft's data-handling terms before allowing Copilot to process that data.
- **Content angle:** Carousel (how-to) · System teaching · hook: "Your Excel spreadsheet is messier than you think. Copilot just fixed it in 10 seconds."
