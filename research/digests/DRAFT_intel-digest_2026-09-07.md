## Top 3 this week

### 1. ChatGPT can now open and edit your Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides without leaving the chat

- **What changed:** OpenAI rolled out live Google Drive integration so that paid ChatGPT users can open, read, and edit any Google Doc, Sheet, or Slide directly inside the ChatGPT chat window. The file stays connected to Drive, so edits go back to the real document without any copy-pasting.
- **Source + date:** 9to5Mac ("ChatGPT subscribers can now open and edit Google Drive files from inside the chat"), August 14, 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** If your business lives in Google Workspace, your AI assistant now lives there too. Draft a client proposal, update your bookings spreadsheet, or tidy a slide deck by asking ChatGPT in plain English, while the file is right there in the same window. No toggling tabs, no pasting text blocks back and forth.
- **Honest caveat:** This requires a paid ChatGPT plan: Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise. Free accounts have no app connections at all. Plus users are subject to an agentic usage limit each month, so heavy daily use may hit a ceiling. Mobile support has not shipped yet.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Your ChatGPT and your Google Drive are finally in the same room."

### 2. Gemini in Google Workspace now remembers how you like to communicate

- **What changed:** Google expanded persistent custom instructions to the Gemini side panel across Gmail, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and Drive. You set your preferences once, and Gemini carries them into every session across all of those apps. No re-prompting, no context copy-paste.
- **Source + date:** Google Workspace Updates blog ("Custom instructions for Gemini in Workspace now available in more apps"), September 2, 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** Every Workspace user who already pays for Gemini access can now tell it "write in a warm, direct tone, keep emails under 150 words, never use jargon" once, and have that stick across every app. It is the difference between a tool that learns you and one you have to train from scratch every morning.
- **Honest caveat:** This requires a Google Workspace subscription with Gemini access included: it is not on every plan and not on the standalone Gemini app. The rollout is gradual and may take up to 15 days to reach your account. Instructions apply within eligible Workspace apps only. You can save up to 1,000 individual instructions.
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Stop re-explaining yourself to Gemini every morning."

### 3. Xero's AI now predicts when your invoices will be paid, and chases them automatically

- **What changed:** Xero released significant updates to JAX (Just Ask Xero) on September 3, adding two new capabilities: payment-date forecasting, where JAX analyses your historical payment patterns and predicts when each open invoice is likely to be paid, then sends automated payment reminders based on those predictions; and automated expense processing, where JAX handles employee expense claim review and categorisation without manual steps. JAX can also now generate financial reports in response to plain-English questions.
- **Source + date:** TechRepublic ("Xero's JAX Now Smarter at Predicting When Customers Will Pay") / Xero media release, September 3, 2026.
- **So what for the owner:** Late-paying clients are one of the top cash-flow headaches for service businesses, including health, wellness, and clinic owners. JAX can now flag which invoices are at risk of running late before they do, and send reminders automatically, so you are not the one awkwardly following up. For any owner with staff submitting receipts, automated expense processing removes a recurring admin block.
- **Honest caveat:** Requires a Xero subscription with JAX access. Payment-prediction accuracy depends on having enough payment history in your Xero account; a new business with thin history will get less reliable forecasts. Beta status on some features means behaviour may change. *(Regulated-claim flag: finance. Frame any resulting post as a time and admin win. Do not claim it replaces an accountant, provides tax advice, or guarantees cash-flow outcomes.)*
- **Content angle:** Carousel · System teaching · hook: "Xero can now tell you which clients are likely to pay late, before they do."
