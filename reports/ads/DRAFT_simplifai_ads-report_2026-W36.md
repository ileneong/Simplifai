## 3. Derived formulas (for manual calculation)

- **Frequency** = Impressions ÷ Reach
- **CPM** = Amount spent ÷ Impressions × 1,000
- **Cost per recall lift** = Amount spent ÷ Estimated ad recall lift (people)
- **Engagement rate (%)** = Post engagements ÷ Reach × 100

## 4. What good looks like (benchmarks)

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | Lower is better | Compare creatives; cheapest reach wins |
| Frequency | Under ~1.8–2.0 in a short window | Climbing frequency + climbing CPM = fatigue → refresh |
| Est. recall lift | Higher (lower cost per recall) | Signals the ad is memorable, not just seen |
| Saves / shares / profile visits | Secondary tiebreakers | Use when CPM is similar across creatives |
