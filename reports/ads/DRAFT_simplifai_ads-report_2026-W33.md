## What to look for (awareness objective)

- **CPM** — lower is better; cheapest reach wins.
- **Frequency** — healthy target: under ~1.8–2.0 in a short window. Climbing frequency + rising CPM = fatigue → refresh creative or widen audience.
- **Estimated ad recall lift** — higher and cheaper cost-per-recall = more memorable, not just seen.
- **Saves / shares / profile visits** — secondary tiebreakers between creatives with similar CPM.
