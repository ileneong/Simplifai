## 4. What good looks like (awareness — no pixel)

- **CPM** — lower is better; cheapest reach wins between creatives.
- **Frequency** — healthy roughly under ~1.8–2.0 in a short window. Rising frequency + rising CPM → creative fatigue → **refresh** or widen audience.
- **Estimated ad recall lift** — higher recall (lower cost per recall) = ad is actually memorable, not just seen.
- **Saves / shares / profile visits** — secondary tiebreakers between creatives with similar CPM.
- **Cost per follow** — derived from total spend ÷ new follows; tracks brand growth efficiency.
